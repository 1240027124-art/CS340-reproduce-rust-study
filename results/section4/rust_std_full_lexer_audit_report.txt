LOC methodology:
Function intervals are merged separately within each Rust source file.
Intervals from different source files are never merged.
Raw LOC is the sum of all detected function interval lengths.
Unique LOC is the sum of merged interval lengths within each file.
Duplicate LOC is Raw LOC minus Unique LOC.
